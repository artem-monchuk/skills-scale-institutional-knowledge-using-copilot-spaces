# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation repository. This is the central hub for all program management processes, workflows, and best practices used by OctoAcme teams.

## Brief Overview of OctoAcme's Project Management Processes

OctoAcme employs a structured yet adaptable framework for managing projects, emphasizing clear roles, iterative workflows, and continuous communication. Project management starts with well-defined phases: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Each project is kicked off by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and setting measurable goals using a “Project One-pager.” Planning then breaks down deliverables into a prioritized backlog, estimates scope, clarifies responsibilities, and identifies dependencies, all supported by standard templates and checklists to ensure alignment and risk mitigation.

Within these workflows, distinct personas drive project momentum. Project Managers coordinate day-to-day execution, manage risks, and maintain timelines and documentation. Product Managers shape the "what" and "why" by setting outcomes and prioritizing the backlog based on customer and business value. Developers implement features to meet acceptance criteria, participate in code reviews, and uphold code quality. QA specialists validate releases against requirements, while stakeholders provide feedback and guide go/no-go decisions.

Communication is both regular and intentional. Daily standups address progress and blockers, with weekly delivery syncs and monthly stakeholder updates to ensure transparency. Key artifacts—such as roadmaps, risk registers, and status reports—are maintained in a single source of truth, often the project repository itself. Stakeholder communication leverages standardized templates, and escalation paths are clearly defined—from team triage to sponsor-level intervention for critical issues.

Quality assurance is embedded at every stage. Automated tests and security scans are required in CI before code is merged, and manual QA verifies features prior to release. Each release follows a checklist—covering acceptance criteria, backups, smoke tests, deployment, and communication—to reduce risk and improve observability. Retrospectives after milestones focus on capturing learnings and translating them into actionable process improvements, tracked through the backlog and reviewed regularly, thus sustaining a culture of continuous improvement.

## Purpose

These documents provide standardized guidance for managing projects across OctoAcme, ensuring consistent communication, risk management, and delivery practices. They serve as both onboarding material for new team members and a reference for ongoing project execution.

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily:** Standups (progress, blockers)
- **Weekly:** PM + PdM sync; delivery team syncs
- **Monthly:** Stakeholder updates
- **As needed:** Risk escalation and incident communication

## Key Principles

- Transparency: Share status, risks, and decisions with all stakeholders
- Accountability: Clear ownership at all levels
- Flexibility: Adapt processes to project size and evolving needs
- Learning: Continuous improvement through retrospectives and action items
